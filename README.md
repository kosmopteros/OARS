# OARS

**Open Artificial Respondent Standard**

**Version 1.0**

OARS defines the category **Artificial Respondent**: what it is, what it is
not, and the minimum conditions under which a response may be attributed to
one.

> **The respondent exists before the particular audience query, panel
> selection, or task that later finds it. The response is causally traceable to
> what that respondent already was and to what it actually observed.**

An Artificial Respondent is not a persona, a demographic row, a model, or a
generated answer. `Artificial` describes the origin of the respondent;
`synthetic` and `synthesized` describe provenance or production acts. Neither
synthesis nor plausible role-play establishes respondenthood.

OARS 1.0 is deliberately small. It defines the category and its eight
requirements. It does not prescribe an LLM, a generation method, a data
format, a validator, or a certification system.
